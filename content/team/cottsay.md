Scott is a software engineer based in Minnesota in the United States.
He currently works on ROS infrastructure at Intrinsic and has previously worked for Open Robotics and Microsoft.
Scott began using ROS shortly after the release of Electric Emys and eventually became the "ROS Boss" for the ROS 2 releases of Galactic Geochelone and Kilted Kaiju many years later.

Before entering the software industry, Scott attended the South Dakota School of Mines and Technology earning a Master's Degree in Computational Science and Robotics.

Scott currently serves as the Project Leader for the Infrastructure PMC.
